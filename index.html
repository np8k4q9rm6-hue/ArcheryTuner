Getting Started

How It Works

  • 1 Shoot an arrow at 20 yards into your target.
  • 2 Open the app and tap Analyze Shot. Select where your arrow hit and how the nock was angled in the target.
  • 3 Archery Tuner diagnoses the issue — fishtailing, porpoising, nock high/low — and gives you step-by-step fix instructions.
  • 4 Make the adjustment, shoot again, and repeat until your arrows fly true.
FAQ

Common Questions

What distance should I shoot from?

20 yards is the recommended distance for paper tuning. It gives enough flight time to reveal tuning issues without too much environmental interference.

What does "nock orientation" mean?

When your arrow hits the target, the tail (nock end) may be angled up, down, left, or right rather than straight. This angle tells us a lot about what's happening during flight.

Does this work for both compound and recurve bows?

Yes — the tuning principles apply to both. The fix instructions are general enough to work across bow types.

How many shots can I log?

You can store up to 20 recent shots in the app.
