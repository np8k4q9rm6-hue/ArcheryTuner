# 🎯 Archery Tuner — Support

**Archery Tuner** helps beginner archers dial in their shot. Tell the app where your arrow hit and the nock orientation — get a diagnosis and step-by-step fix instructions.

---

## How It Works

1. **Shoot** an arrow at 20 yards into your target
2. **Open the app** and tap Analyze Shot
3. **Select** where your arrow hit and how the nock was angled in the target
4. **Get a diagnosis** — fishtailing, porpoising, nock high/low, and more
5. **Follow the fix** — simple step-by-step instructions to correct your tuning

---

## FAQ

**What distance should I shoot from?**
20 yards is recommended. It gives enough flight time to reveal tuning issues without too much environmental interference.

**What does "nock orientation" mean?**
When your arrow hits the target, the tail (nock end) may be angled up, down, left, or right rather than flying straight. This angle tells the app what's happening during flight.

**Does this work for compound and recurve bows?**
Yes — the tuning principles apply to both bow types.

**How many shots can I log?**
Up to 20 recent shots are stored in the app.
